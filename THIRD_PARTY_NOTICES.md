## Icons, fonts, and media

- Interface icons are simple SVG paths implemented in the source. No remote icon or brand asset service is used.
- The application uses a device system font stack and distributes no font binaries.
- Files under `apps/web/public/demo/` are geometric images, test audio, and short videos generated by `apps/web/scripts/generate-demo-media.mjs`. Per-file hashes, dimensions, durations, audio flags, and clip provenance are recorded in `apps/web/public/demo/MEDIA_MANIFEST.json`.
- FFmpeg and ffprobe are optional local generation and integrity-check tools. Their binaries are not distributed here; redistribution of a chosen build requires a separate license review.
- Playwright browser binaries are installed into `.cache/ms-playwright/` and are ignored rather than distributed.
